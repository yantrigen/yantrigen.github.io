MechScribe

App Development UI/UX Design EdTech Utility

Project Overview

MechScribe is a centralized mobile application designed specifically to solve a core problem for engineering students: organizing and accessing study materials, syllabi, and practical designs in one digital workspace. It acts as a bridge between mechanical engineering needs and digital convenience.

Key Features

  • Centralized Database: Quick access to categorized PDF notes, CAD models, and assignment briefs.
  • Intuitive UI: A dark-themed, distraction-free user interface tailored for long study hours.
  • Resource Management: Helps students track their syllabus completion and upcoming lab practicals.
