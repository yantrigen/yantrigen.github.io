RoboArm Mk.I

Kinematics Mechanical Design Automation

Project Overview

The RoboArm Mk.I is a functional prototype of a robotic arm designed for automated pick-and-place operations. It represents a deep dive into mechanical design principles and demonstrates the practical application of industrial robotics on a smaller scale.

Engineering Focus

  • Inverse Kinematics: Calculating the exact joint angles required to move the end-effector (gripper) to a specific target coordinate in 3D space.
  • Structural Stability: Utilizing rigid materials and optimized geometry to minimize vibration and deflection during movement.
  • Actuation: Integrated servo motors at the base, shoulder, elbow, and wrist joints for smooth, controlled multi-axis motion.
