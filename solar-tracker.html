Project Overview

The Smart Solar Tracker is a mechanical and electronic integration project designed to automatically orient solar panels towards the sun. By constantly adjusting the panel's angle throughout the day, this system maximizes energy absorption compared to static solar panels.

How It Works (Kinematics & Control)

Using Light Dependent Resistors (LDRs) positioned on the panel, the system detects the intensity of sunlight. An Arduino microcontroller processes this data and commands servo motors to rotate the mechanical axis, ensuring the panel is always perpendicular to the sun's rays.

  • Sensors: 4 LDRs for multi-directional light sensing.
  • Microcontroller: Arduino UNO for logic processing.
  • Actuators: High-torque servo motors for precise angular movement.
  • Structure: Custom-designed mechanical frame for stability.
