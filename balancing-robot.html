Self-Balancing Robot

Robotics PID Controller MPU6050 Control Systems

Project Overview

The Self-Balancing Robot is an advanced robotics project utilizing sensors and control algorithms to maintain equilibrium and stability on two wheels. Similar to an inverted pendulum, it constantly reads its orientation and drives the motors to prevent itself from falling over.

How It Works (Kinematics & Control)

The core of this robot lies in its feedback loop. By mathematically calculating the tilt error, the system applies the exact amount of force needed in the opposite direction.

  • IMU Sensor (MPU6050): Combines a 3-axis accelerometer and a 3-axis gyroscope to precisely measure the robot's tilt angle in real-time.
  • PID Algorithm: A custom-tuned Proportional-Integral-Derivative controller processes the sensor data to calculate the required motor speed.
  • Microcontroller: An Arduino board runs the high-speed calculations and sends PWM signals to the motor drivers.
  • Actuators: DC Gear Motors (or Steppers) provide the fast response time needed to keep the chassis upright.
