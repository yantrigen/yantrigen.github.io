Who We Are

About Yantrigen Labs

Yantrigen Labs is a modern technology initiative and student-led robotics workspace. We specialize in Artificial Intelligence, Mechanical Engineering designs, and Robotics automation. Our focus is on building smart, real-world tech solutions.

Meet the Founder

Hi, I am Shubham Gaikwad, a 19-year-old Mechanical Engineering student and an aspiring Robotics Design Engineer. My passion lies in merging physical machines with digital intelligence. From CAD designs to functional automated prototypes, my goal is to push the boundaries of student-led innovation.

Our Vision & Roadmap

Our projects range from Smart Solar Trackers and Self-Balancing Robots to utility apps like MechScribe. Looking ahead, my roadmap includes cracking the GATE exam to pursue an M.Tech in Robotics, ultimately scaling Yantrigen Labs into a full-fledged tech startup that solves industry challenges.
