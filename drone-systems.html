Robotics and Drone Systems

UAV Aerodynamics Flight Controller

Project Overview

This project focuses on the integration of aerodynamics with intelligent control systems to build versatile Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs). The objective is to design drone systems capable of stable flight, automated mapping, and payload delivery for modern real-world applications.

Technical Details

  • Flight Dynamics: Balancing thrust, weight, lift, and drag using calculated propeller configurations.
  • Control System: Implementing PID tuning on the flight controller to ensure stability against wind resistance.
  • Sensors: Utilizing GPS, Barometers, and Gyroscopes for accurate positioning and altitude hold.
